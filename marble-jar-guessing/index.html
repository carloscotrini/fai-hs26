What this page knows about you

Nothing, and it asks you for nothing. It picks four letters at random on your phone, mixes them into one number and reads a row off that number. Nothing is sent anywhere, this page has no network connection of its own, and the sheet only ever holds four letters and a number of marbles. Many codes lead to the same row, so a row says nothing about who you are.
